    Privacy Policy

    Effective date: October 29, 2026 · Last updated: October 29, 2026

    This Privacy Policy explains how TechStack Lab (“TechStack Lab”, “we”, “us”) collects, uses and shares information when you visit techstacklab.site (the “Site”). We aim to collect as little personal data as possible and to be clear about the data that third parties, especially advertising partners, collect.

    1. Who is responsible for your data

    TechStack Lab is the data controller for personal data processed through the Site. Contact: support@techstacklab.com.

    2. Information we collect

    Information you give us

    • Contact form and email: your name, email address, topic and message content.
    • Newsletter: your email address and subscription preferences, if you subscribe. The sign-up form is submitted securely and your address is never placed in page URLs.

    Information collected automatically

    • Technical data: IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, referring page, pages visited and timestamps, collected in server logs by our hosting provider for security and reliability.
    • Cookies and similar technologies: described in section 4. Non-essential cookies are only set with your consent where the law requires it.

    We do not knowingly collect special categories of personal data, and we do not ask for payment information.

    3. How we use information and our legal bases

    4. Cookies, advertising and Google AdSense

    Cookies are small text files stored on your device. We use the following categories:

    • Strictly necessary and functional: remember your theme choice, your cookie consent decision, and the date of your last visit (so the homepage can highlight what changed since then). These are stored only in your browser's local storage, are never sent to us or anyone else, and do not track you across sites.
    • Analytics (optional): help us understand which articles are read, in aggregate.
    • Advertising (optional): set by Google and its partners to serve, limit the frequency of, and measure ads.

    Google AdSense

    We use Google AdSense to display advertisements. In connection with this:

    • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.
    • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this Site and/or other sites on the Internet.
    • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info/choices (US) or www.youronlinechoices.eu (EU).
    • Third-party vendors and ad networks other than Google may also serve ads on the Site. A list of these vendors, with links to their privacy policies and opt-out options, is available through the consent message shown to visitors in regions where it applies, and on request.

    Learn more about how Google uses data from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

    The DoubleClick (DART) cookie

    Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies — historically known as the DoubleClick DART cookie, now operated under the doubleclick.net and Google domains — to serve ads to visitors based on their visits to this Site and other sites on the Internet. These cookies help show more relevant ads, limit how often you see the same ad, and measure ad performance. You can opt out of the use of these cookies for personalized ads through Google's Ads Settings.

    Consent and Google Consent Mode

    When you first visit, we ask for your choices about analytics, advertising and ad personalization. Until you choose, advertising and analytics storage is set to “denied” using Google Consent Mode. If you decline advertising cookies, Google may still show non-personalized or limited ads that do not rely on cookies for personalization. Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland may be shown a consent message provided through Google's certified consent management platform.

    You can change your choices at any time using the link in the footer of every page, or by clearing cookies and site data in your browser. We store your choice for up to 12 months and then ask again.

    5. How we share information

    We do not sell your personal information for money. We share data only with:

    • Service providers who host the Site, deliver email and process form submissions on our behalf, under contracts that limit their use of the data.
    • Advertising partners, including Google, as described in section 4, based on your choices.
    • Authorities where required by law or to protect our rights and users.

    6. Your rights under the GDPR and UK GDPR

    If you are in the EEA, UK or Switzerland, you have the right to: access your personal data; correct inaccurate data; request deletion; restrict or object to processing, including processing based on legitimate interests; data portability; and withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier processing. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

    To exercise these rights, email support@techstacklab.com or use the privacy request form. We respond within one month.

    7. Your rights under the CCPA/CPRA (California) and other US state laws

    If you are a resident of California or another US state with a comprehensive privacy law, you have the right to:

    • Know the categories and specific pieces of personal information we collect, use and disclose;
    • Request deletion and correction of your personal information;
    • Opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising;
    • Limit the use of sensitive personal information (we do not collect it); and
    • Not be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

    Advertising cookies set by third parties on the Site may be considered “sharing” under California law. To opt out, select “Reject non-essential” or turn off “Ad personalization” in . We honor Global Privacy Control (GPC) browser signals as a valid opt-out request. In the past 12 months, the categories collected are identifiers (such as IP address and email), internet activity, and inferences used for advertising.

    To submit a request, email support@techstacklab.com with the subject “Privacy request”. We will verify your request and respond within 45 days. You may use an authorized agent.

    8. Residents of the United Arab Emirates

    If you are in the UAE, the Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data (PDPL) may apply to our processing of your data. Subject to that law, you may request access to your personal data and information about how it is processed, ask us to correct or erase it, restrict or stop processing, object to processing for direct marketing or profiling, and receive your data in a portable format. Visitors in the DIFC or ADGM free zones may have equivalent rights under those zones' own data protection laws.

    Send requests to support@techstacklab.com. Where we rely on consent (newsletter, analytics, advertising cookies), you can withdraw it at any time.

    9. Data retention

    Contact messages are kept for up to 24 months and then deleted. Newsletter data is kept until you unsubscribe. Server logs are kept for up to 90 days. Advertising cookie lifetimes are set by Google and its partners and are described in their policies.

    10. International transfers

    Our service providers and advertising partners may process data outside your country, including in the United States. Where required, these transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or an adequacy decision.

    11. Security

    The Site is served over HTTPS, and access to the systems that store your messages is restricted and protected with multi-factor authentication. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

    12. Children

    The Site is intended for professional audiences and is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with data, contact us and we will delete it.

    13. Changes to this policy

    We may update this policy as our practices or the law change. We will post the updated version on this page with a new “Last updated” date and, for material changes, show a notice on the Site.

    14. Contact

    Questions about this policy: support@techstacklab.com or our contact form.
