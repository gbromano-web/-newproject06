4. Cookies, advertising and Google AdSense

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. We use the following categories:

  • Strictly necessary and functional: remember your theme choice, your cookie consent decision, and the date of your last visit (so the homepage can highlight what changed since then). These are stored only in your browser's local storage, are never sent to us or anyone else, and do not track you across sites.
  • Analytics (optional): help us understand which articles are read, in aggregate.
  • Advertising (optional): set by Google and its partners to serve, limit the frequency of, and measure ads.

Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements. In connection with this:

Learn more about how Google uses data from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

The DoubleClick (DART) cookie

Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies — historically known as the DoubleClick DART cookie, now operated under the doubleclick.net and Google domains — to serve ads to visitors based on their visits to this Site and other sites on the Internet.
